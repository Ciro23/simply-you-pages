Privacy Policy for Simply You

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Simply You is a personal finance tracker app. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

We do not collect any data

Simply You does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, financial data, or usage data. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services built into the app.

Where your data lives

All information you enter into the app — accounts, transactions, categories, and settings — is stored locally on your own device only, in a private local database. It is never sent to us or to any third party. Nothing leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it yourself (for example, exporting a CSV file), and that export stays under your control.

No third parties

Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing to share with advertisers, analytics providers, or any other third party.

Children's privacy

Simply You does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated accordingly, and the effective date above will be revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to ciro.sorrentino@pm.me.
