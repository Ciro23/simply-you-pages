Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
Everything you enter stays on your device only, in a local database. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Do I need an internet connection?
No. Simply You works fully offline.

How do I back up or move my data to a new phone?
Use the app's export feature to save your data to a file you control, then import it on your new device.
